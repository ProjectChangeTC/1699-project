About Panjab Life

Wear the history. Learn the history.

Panjab Life is heritage streetwear for the Punjabi generation — Gen Z and the young diaspora who want to carry Sikh history with pride. Every design is rooted in real events, real places, and real ideals: the five rivers, the birth of the Khalsa in 1699, the stand at Chamkaur, the 21 of Saragarhi, the saint-soldier path, and the eternal rising spirit of Chardi Kala.

We present Sikhism and the Sikh warrior ethos (Sant-Sipahi) with dignity and historical accuracy. Symbols, Gurmukhi calligraphy, dates, and artistic illustrations of historical warriors replace stereotypes. No faces of the Gurus appear on our garments or imagery — only the symbols and stories that belong to the community.

The product is a history lesson you can wear. The Heritage section is as important as the shop. Oversized silhouettes, heavyweight fabrics, gold-foil accents, and full-garment compositions keep the focus on quality and storytelling.

Extended sizing from XS to 3XL from day one. Men’s oversized and women’s boyfriend/cropped fits are designed separately, not simply scaled. Every piece links back to its story.
